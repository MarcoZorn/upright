# Amendment 1 to the pre-registration (2026-11-04)

Added before any off-Earth training. [PREREG.md](PREREG.md) is unchanged.

**Why:** on Earth, both trained arms already reach speed with an in-phase, two-footed bound. Plain MLP Y gen 1869 has an inter-leg phase of ≈ 0.6 rad at 2 m/s; CPG run X has Δφ ≈ 0.16. So hopping at low gravity, on its own, would not show that gravity changed the gait.

**Change to H2:** H2 is now judged against the same seed's Earth policy *at matched Froude number*. It holds if, at g = 1.62, |Δφ − π| or the skip rate is larger than on Earth at the same Fr, by at least 0.5 rad or 0.15 respectively, in at least 3 of 5 seeds. H1 and the kill criteria are unchanged.
